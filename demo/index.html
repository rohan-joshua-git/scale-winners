      How TrustSphere works

      Eight phases, one auditable path from raw ledger to a human decision.

      Every score traces back through the same pipeline — nothing skips a step, and nothing downstream of Phase 6 is allowed to decide on its own.

      1. 1

        Raw data ingestion

        Synthetic transactions and a KYC/case-note corpus, seeded with the outliers and patterns the demo needs to find — nothing is left to chance.

      2. 2

        Data quality checks

        NaN, duplicate, and reporting-gap audits run before any scoring — a missing field or a suspicious gap is itself a signal, not noise to discard.

      3. 3
        SAP Datasphere

        Federation

        NA, EU, and APAC sources unify into one queryable layer — a semantic layer over each region's existing core banking system, not a migration.

      4. 4
        SAP BTP

        Scoring & risk tiering

        An AHP-weighted rule score blends with Isolation Forest, EWMA, and GARCH signals; typology tagging and the severity matrix decide the final tier.

      5. 5

        Context retrieval (RAG)

        Vector search over KYC and policy documents, plus beneficial-ownership graph traversal for shell-company cases, surfaces the context behind a flag.

      6. 6
        Joule

        Explainability

        Score, factors, and retrieved context become a two-to-three sentence rationale a compliance officer can act on. Joule explains — it never decides.

      7. 7

        Outputs & dashboards

        An investigator case screen surfaces the score, tier, factors, and rationale; leadership KPIs — false-positive rate, cost-per-case — roll up separately.

      8. 8

        Human review

        An analyst accepts or overrides the tier with a mandatory reason, logged to an append-only audit trail. No score is final without it.

      Beneficial-ownership graph

      Unstructured records. One traceable structure.

      On their own, four company registrations and a wire transfer look unrelated. Graph traversal over beneficial-ownership records resolves them into a single structure in seconds.

      Beneficial-ownership graph traversal links the beneficiary to 3 other shell entities registered within 11 days of each other, all controlled by a single ultimate beneficial owner.

      Phase 4e — Typology & severity matrix

      Likelihood and severity, not one score, decide the tier.

      Two independent bands look up a tier in a fixed matrix — transparent and auditable, not a black-box threshold. Sanctions hits bypass it entirely via a hard override straight to Critical.
